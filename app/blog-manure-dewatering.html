Cow and Pig Manure Dewatering: Choose by Bedding and End Use

Sand-bedded free-stall barns: use the CXXY mesh-belt-free press — it tolerates sand wear that destroys ordinary screws.

Standard barns: the CXCG super high-pressure press takes fresh manure at 75–80% moisture to 50±2%, and the fibrous cake can be composted or dried as fuel.

Pig farms: CXGY screw separator for raw manure; add the CXTS high-dry press if you compost.

In all cases, separated liquid goes to the wastewater system — size it before you order the machine.
