CXOF, CXQF or CXPF: Which DAF Fits Your Wastewater?

All three are dissolved air flotation units, but they are built for different jobs. CXOF suits oily wastewater and tight spaces; CXQF handles high flow in a shallow round tank; CXPF is the common choice for general industrial pretreatment.

Read more

From 80% to 60% Moisture: Three Levels of Sludge Dewatering

A standard belt filter press reaches 25–35% cake dryness. For incineration or composting, the ultra-high-pressure belt press (CXCG) reaches 60% or lower, and the thin-layer high-dry press (CXBC) reaches 40–50% with no lime.

Cow and Pig Manure Dewatering: Choose by Bedding and End Use

Sand-bedded barns need the CXXY mesh-belt-free press; standard barns use the CXCG press to reach 50±2% moisture. Pig farms start with the CXGY screw separator and add the CXTS press for composting.
