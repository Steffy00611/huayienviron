CXOF, CXQF or CXPF: Which DAF Fits Your Wastewater?

All three are dissolved air flotation units, but they are built for different jobs.

CXOF is a compact rectangular unit, 5–80 m³/h, with flotation and sedimentation in one tank. It suits oily wastewater and food processing lines where space is short.

CXQF is a round shallow tank, 1,500–10,000 m³/d, with 600–750 mm water depth and 2–3 minutes retention. Papermaking and textile plants choose it for high flow and fast flotation.

CXPF is the rectangular 4–300 m³/h unit for surface-water clarification, fiber recovery and sludge thickening; it is the most common choice for general industrial pretreatment.
