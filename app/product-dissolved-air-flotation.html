Dissolved Air Flotation Units (CXOF / CXQF / CXPF)

Patented two-in-one flotation and clarification units for oil, suspended solids and fiber removal.

Patent 201720174028.6 / 201720174147.1CE Marked

CXQF &amp; CXPF are CE marked.

Three designs for the same job — removing oil, suspended solids and light particles that settle slowly.

CXOF (5–80 m³/h): flotation and sedimentation in one tank, suited to oily and food wastewater.

CXQF (1,500–10,000 m³/d): round shallow tank, 600–750 mm water depth, 2–3 minutes retention, for papermaking and textile wastewater. [Patent 201720174028.6]

CXPF (4–300 m³/h): rectangular steel tank for surface water clarification, fiber recovery and sludge thickening, used in petroleum, chemical, printing & dyeing, leather, steel, starch and food industries. [Patents 201720174028.6 / 201720174147.1]
