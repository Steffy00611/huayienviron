Lamella Sedimentation Tank (CXLML)

60° inclined plates, 10–100 m³/h in about half the footprint of a conventional tank.

Uses 60° inclined plates to multiply the settling area. Hydraulic loading 15–25 m³/m²·h; footprint about half that of a conventional sedimentation tank of the same capacity. Sludge slides down the plates by gravity into the hopper. Models CXLML-10 to CXLML-100 (10–100 m³/h). Standard plate spacing 80–100 mm (100–120 mm for high turbidity); backwash every 2–5 days. Carbon steel with anti-corrosion coating or stainless steel.
